# Mission OS 6.0

Mission OS turns DaemonCore from a large catalog into a deliberate operating curriculum. It answers three questions every time the app opens: where the operator stands, what role they are building toward, and what work should happen next.

## What is real in alpha 1

- A 12-decision entry diagnostic covering six security domains
- Six selectable professional pathways
- Persistent scores, selected route, and timestamps in the local operator record
- Route progress derived from recorded Academy, Lab Range, Web Forge, Enterprise Forge, and Mastery work
- Three seeded flagship case emphases for Ghost Port, Broken Trust, and Night Shift
- A sealed after-action review measuring evidence coverage, independence, method discipline, and time discipline
- Concrete remediation and next-action guidance after every completed adaptive range
- Visible version and platform identity in the running product

## Product boundary

The diagnostic is a training baseline, not an employment guarantee or third-party certification. Mission OS does not invent cohort comparisons. Readiness and progress signals are derived only from evidence stored on the current device.

The range remains disposable and egress-denied. Case variation changes the professional analysis emphasis while the underlying objective contract remains deterministic and reviewable.

## Release gate

6.0 should not replace the stable installer until the full test suite, Windows packaging, clean-machine upgrade, Docker range smoke tests, receipt export, and Linux packaging pass. Alpha builds should be clearly labeled and published separately from stable.
