## Buyer FAQ

### What does the purchase unlock?

It unlocks FieldOps Pro inside the DaemonCore Academy desktop application: signed engagements, diagnostics, campaigns, sealed evidence, findings, retests, professional exports, and controlled Chaos Engine workflows. Academy training remains free.

### Is this a subscription?

No. This variant is a one-time purchase with no recurring subscription. The license remains subject to the EULA, device allowance, and continued compliance with the product terms.

### How many computers can I use?

One active device at a time. Deactivate the old device from Settings before activating a replacement.

### Does the license authorize security testing?

No. It unlocks the software only. FieldOps requires a separate signed engagement permit, and the operator is responsible for valid written authorization from the system owner.

### Does FieldOps run arbitrary attacks?

No. Real-system operations use bounded, purpose-built diagnostics and resilience profiles enforced against the signed target, port, network, time, and policy boundary. Unrestricted experimentation belongs inside disposable, contained Academy ranges.

### Does it work offline?

Initial activation and periodic validation require internet access. A successfully validated license can enter a 14-day offline grace period.

### What platforms are supported?

Windows x64 is stable. Linux x64 is currently distributed as a 6.0 beta for Ubuntu and Debian-family desktops and requires a working GNOME Keyring or KWallet backend for protected FieldOps credentials.

### Do I need Docker Desktop?

Docker Desktop is required for disposable Academy range missions. FieldOps operations may use local Nmap, a pinned Docker toolchain, or the bounded built-in fallback depending on the selected module.

### Where is customer data stored?

Operator progress, engagements, evidence, and licensing metadata are local-first on the customer’s device. License activation communicates with Lemon Squeezy. Customers should export important case files before device changes or major upgrades.

### What is the refund policy?

Refund requests are handled under the posted store policy and applicable consumer law. Direct customers to https://academy.daemoncore.app/support. Do not promise a categorical refund outcome in checkout copy until the final policy has received legal review.
