## Full product description

FieldOps Pro is the commercial operations workspace inside DaemonCore Academy. It turns a signed scope into a traceable engagement—from target inventory and posture evidence through findings, retests, and client-ready delivery.

Every operation starts with a named operator, an approving authority, an exact target boundary, declared ports, a testing window, and an Observe, Validate, or Stress policy. The license unlocks the workspace. The signed engagement authorizes the target.

### Included with FieldOps Pro

- **DaemonCore Trust Authority** — device-protected Ed25519 operator identity, signed engagement permits, named operation receipts, and tamper-evident audit history.
- **Campaign Engine** — durable Complete Assessment, Service Inventory, and Change Verification campaigns across up to 100 explicitly authorized targets.
- **Deep Service Inventory** — structured service, version, product, confidence, CPE, state, and reason evidence through local Nmap, a pinned Docker fallback, or the built-in passive profiler.
- **Surface and change intelligence** — bounded DNS, HTTP, TLS, service, and response baselines that expose material drift between captures.
- **Sealed evidence vault** — persistent raw captures bound to target, time, resolved addresses, engagement, and SHA-256 integrity.
- **Findings and retests** — reviewed severity, impact, remediation, disposition, and evidence-backed closure history.
- **Professional delivery** — printable HTML reports, machine-readable JSON case files, and a chained activity ledger.
- **Chaos Engine** — controlled resilience sampling with fixed ceilings, live SLO aborts, emergency stop, recovery measurement, and sealed results.
- **Protected licensing** — operating-system credential storage, device deactivation, periodic validation, and a 14-day offline grace period.

### Responsible-use boundary

FieldOps Pro is professional software for systems you own or are explicitly authorized to assess. A purchase does not grant permission to test any system. Operators remain responsible for written authorization, scope, timing, safety limits, and applicable law.

DaemonCore Trust Authority provides device-key attribution and tamper-evident records. It does not independently verify that a typed identity or authorization claim is truthful.
